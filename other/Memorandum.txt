SUBJECT: Presentation Structure and Content Requirements for "The Future of Clubs"

PROJECT OVERVIEW AND TECHNICAL FORMAT
We are preparing a comprehensive presentation titled "The Future of Clubs" to document our history, showcase our current achievements, and outline the future trajectory of student organizations at University Laboratory School and College. 

This presentation will not use traditional software like PowerPoint. It is being built as a custom web application using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. This approach allows us to create a highly polished, interactive, and modern slide deck that functions exactly like a presentation but utilizes the full capabilities of modern web browsers. It will feature smooth transitions, custom typography, and responsive layouts.

GUIDELINES FOR EXECUTIVE INPUT
To ensure accuracy and maintain focus, please adhere to the following rules when reviewing and providing content for your respective sections:

1. Scope of Authority: You are free to express opinions, adjust narratives, and refine the content specifically for your own club. You may not comment on, edit, or provide directives regarding any other club's section.
2. Slide Count Flexibility: The slide allocations listed below are a baseline. You may increase or decrease the number of slides dedicated to your club's history or future plans, provided the changes remain within reasonable limits. The content must be logical, concise, and technically feasible to code within a modern web framework.
3. Assets: High-resolution logos and images are required. Please ensure all files are named exactly as specified in the outline below.
